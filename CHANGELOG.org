#+title:     Changelog
#+language:  en

* Upcoming
** Added
1. Combat bookkeeping moved out of pop-up dialogs: every encounter control
   except the destructive Reset / Clear confirmation now opens into a panel
   beside the queue instead of over it.  No action needed.
2. The panel editors target the active creature or the current selection; they
   stay open after applying, remember un-applied settings when closed, and keep
   recent-applies logs (undo-able for HP and conditions).  No action needed.
3. When applying a dice-formula HP change to a selection, a "New roll for each
   creature" option rolls independently per creature.  No action needed.
4. Creature cards name each special reaction in an orange badge on the card's
   top row, and grey out legendary-action pips during the creature's own turn
   (5e bars using them then).  No action needed.
5. A creature card's status labels open the Status editor targeting that
   creature, while the column's Status button folds the editor away whoever it
   is aimed at.  No action needed.
6. The initiative manager is one of those editors now, opened by the column's
   Initiative button or by a card's initiative circle.  No action needed.
7. The status, condition, replace, and duplicate editors choose their scope with
   a pair of buttons — "Apply to Target" and "Apply to Selected (N)" — rather
   than an apply-scope checkbox.  No action needed.
8. The encounter queue lists its spellcasters in a reminder strip alongside the
   legendary-action and special-reaction ones, and each strip's icon drops down
   a panel detailing what those actions, reactions, or spells do.  No action
   needed.
9. A creature reads as bloodied through red current HP rather than a droplet
   icon.  No action needed.
10. Notes render in italic parentheses on both the creature card and the
    encounter title bar.  No action needed.
11. Card HP values no longer edit in place: clicking one opens the Manage HP
    editor aimed at that creature, whose new Manual section sets the pools
    directly.  No action needed.
12. A creature card's special-reaction badges and its Reaction toggle track each
    other, both clearing at the start of that creature's turn.  No action
    needed.
13. The encounter title bar divides its readouts with separators and lists
    conditions inline, comma-separated.  No action needed.
14. The initiative editor picks how to roll and who to roll for separately,
    replacing its grid of one button per combination.  No action needed.
15. The encounter title bar's flying readout punctuates its height, so the
    number reads as the wing icon's rather than a neighbouring value.  No action
    needed.
16. The Condition/Effect trigger is now labelled Condition.  No action needed.
17. The Encounter Controls and Compendium panes are gone — everything they held,
    down to the dice roller and the pinned stat block, is reached from the
    Actions column on the far left instead.  No action needed.
18. The Compendium always opens in its own browser tab, titled "Compendium",
    with no creature pre-selected; the in-page dialog it used to fall back to is
    gone, and its title-bar "From file" tag went with it.  No action needed.
19. The stat-block panel's title carries a 📖 button that hands the creature off
    to the Compendium tab, opened with that creature selected and scrolled into
    view.  No action needed.
20. Drawer panels stack: opening an editor no longer closes the one already up —
    each new panel opens below it, and Esc closes the newest.  No action needed.
21. A trigger whose panel is open closes it when clicked again, whichever panel
    it is.  No action needed.
22. The Actions column is more compact: smaller buttons, tighter spacing, and a
    slimmer column overall.  No action needed.
23. A denser layout overall: buttons, editor panels, and their spacing all
    tightened, and the drawer column slimmed again, so more of the screen goes
    to the queue.  No action needed.
24. Reset and Clear confirm in a dialog.  No action needed.
25. The Actions column's Reset and Turn buttons read as icons alone, at a size
    that suits them.  No action needed.
26. The round counter in the encounter title bar is clickable, opening a dialog
    that sets the round directly.  No action needed.
27. The Actions column groups its triggers under Creature, Encounter, and
    Compendium headings, with Reset, Turn, and Roll floating above them.  No
    action needed.
28. The XP total, Difficulty, and Treasure moved out of the encounter title bar
    into the Actions column.  No action needed.
29. The Actions column's group headings fold their triggers away when clicked,
    and the column no longer scrolls.  No action needed.
30. Clicking an open drawer panel's heading row folds it down to that row alone,
    so several panels can stay open without filling the column.  No action
    needed.
31. The Manage HP editor sets all three pools from a single Manual row.  No
    action needed.
** Fixes
1. The make-active arrow's hover text no longer repeats the word "active".  No
   action needed.
2. A dirty Save button no longer wears what looks like the open-panel ring — the
   unsaved-changes cue is a yellow border now.  No action needed.
3. A creature card's inline numeric fields close when you click anywhere outside
   them.  No action needed.
4. The Nix build fetches crates again.  No action needed.
5. An encounter never sits at round 0 — new, reset, cleared, and loaded
   encounters all start at round 1.  No action needed.
6. Reset's hover text describes what it actually does — restore HP and clear
   conditions, not revert to the last save.  No action needed.
7. Disclosure carets and the queue's move arrows are large enough to see.  No
   action needed.
8. The Manage HP and Initiative editors no longer leave extra empty space below
   their buttons.  No action needed.
