#+title:     Changelog
#+language:  en

* Upcoming

** Architecture

- Split the Elm frontend into two modules: ~Encounter.elm~ (domain
  layer — types, seed data, turn logic) and ~Main.elm~ (application
  shell + view code).  Domain code does not import rendering
  primitives.  See [[file:docs/ARCHITECTURE.md][docs/ARCHITECTURE.md]] for the full discussion and
  the discipline that goes with it.

** Frontend

- Introduce real turn tracking.  =Encounter.nextTurn= advances the
  active creature in initiative order and increments =round= on wrap.
  Wired to the *⏭ Next Turn* button in Encounter Controls.
- Encounter title bar shows live =round=, active creature name, and
  active creature HP (state icons and the conditions summary stay
  placeholder for now).
- Brand mark dropped; title renders as "eZpZ-dndZ" in plain text
  rather than the gradient mark + wordmark.
- Right-arrow on a creature card now jumps the active marker to that
  creature without advancing the turn (no round bump, no turn-
  progression hooks).  Wires =Encounter.setActive=, distinct from
  =Encounter.nextTurn=.
