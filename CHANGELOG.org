#+title:     Changelog

* Upcoming
** Added
1. Combat bookkeeping moved out of pop-up dialogs: HP, conditions, save chains,
   replacing, and duplicating live in a stationary toolbar whose editors dock
   above the always-visible encounter queue, while timers, memos, and name notes
   are edited directly on the creature card (per the plan in
   =docs/MODAL_MIGRATION.org=).  No action needed.
2. The docked editors target the active creature or, via a checkbox, only the
   selected creatures; they stay open after applying, remember un-applied
   settings when closed, and keep recent-applies logs (undo-able for HP and
   conditions).  No action needed.
3. When applying a dice-formula HP change to a selection, a "New roll for each
   creature" option rolls independently per creature.  No action needed.
4. Creature cards name each special reaction in an orange badge on the card's
   top row, and grey out legendary-action pips during the creature's own turn
   (5e bars using them then).  No action needed.
5. A creature card's status labels are links that open the Status editor
   targeting that creature.  No action needed.
** Fixes
1. The make-active arrow's hover text no longer repeats the word "active".  No
   action needed.
