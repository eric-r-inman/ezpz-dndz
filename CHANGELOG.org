* Upcoming
** Added
1. After applying a condition to the target, the Condition/Effect editor offers
   Apply Changes so it can be adjusted straight away.  No action needed.
2. Next Turn and Make Active clear the highlighted card, and the editors follow
   the active creature.  Highlight a card again to aim them elsewhere.
3. A Condition/Effect log row names the preset it came from, when the preset was
   applied unchanged.  No action needed.
4. The editor column's Next Turn control is wider and the Clear and Reset
   controls beside it are narrower, so the button pressed every round is the
   easiest of the three to hit.  No action needed.
5. The Manage HP editor takes an amount either typed or rolled, from two fields
   side by side, in place of the one field that accepted both.  A formula now
   goes in the Roll field; the greyed-out field is the one that will not commit.
6. The Manage HP editor's Set HP section labels each field with the pool it
   sets.  No action needed.
7. The Set HP section raises a creature's maximum to meet hit points set above
   it, unless a maximum is typed alongside them.  No action needed.
** Fixes
1. The Condition/Effect editor stops offering to change a condition that no
   longer exists, and offers to apply a new one instead.  No action needed.
2. The newest dice result in the editor column shows its flash whole instead of
   clipping at the edge of the row.  No action needed.

   always soaks against the temporary pool first, as 5e requires.  Adjust a

   instead.  The → button that makes a creature active stays.

   longer fits there.  Roll those in the Dice Roller and type the total.

   creature's card, are gone — the current edition dropped the Surprised
   condition.  If you were tracking it for house rules, use a custom
   condition/effect instead.

   Disadvantage picker from a stat block — clicking the ability cell or the
   Saving Throws chip now rolls all three at once instead (see Added).  There's
   no longer a way to fire just one mode from the stat block; use the Dice
   Roller if you need exactly one.
