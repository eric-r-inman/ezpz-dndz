#+title:     Bundled lore groups
#+language:  en

The Random Encounter generator's Lore-leaning toggle draws from a bundled set of
creature compositions, and each composition carries a one-line description that
the Compendium's detail pane shows under the members list.  The Elm module that
ships the bundle is the source of truth for the data.  This document is the
writer's copy: it keeps the reasoning behind each composition, which has no home
in the code, beside the text that ships.

* Voice

The descriptions are table talk rather than stat-block prose: a line the GM can
read aloud as the group appears, in the voice of someone who has run the
encounter and enjoyed it.  A few habits keep new entries in step with the old
ones:

- A sentence or two at most.  The line may explain why these creatures are
  standing together, or it may just enjoy the fact; either way it lands on the
  reader, and "you" is fair game.
- A quip, a warning, or a trailing ellipsis all count as a threat.
- Use plain words.  The vocabulary a text generator leans on (delve, realm,
  tapestry, testament, and their kin) is out.
- Spelling is American, as in the source books.

* Groups

The headings follow the sections in the module.

** Goblinoids

*** Goblinoid Warband

- Id :: =goblinoid-warband=
- Members :: Goblin Warrior ×3–6 (member); Hobgoblin Warrior ×1–2 (leader)
- Why together :: Hobgoblins are the goblinoid soldiery and goblins their
  numerous, expendable kin; Maglubiyet's pecking order puts the one over the
  other.
- Text :: Hobgoblin warriors press goblin levies into a respectable warband —
  the goblins serve as scouts and arrow-fodder for their better-trained elder
  cousins.

*** Goblin Raiding Party

- Id :: =goblin-raid=
- Members :: Goblin Warrior ×3–5 (member); Bugbear Warrior ×1–2 (leader)
- Why together :: Bugbears are the heavies of the goblinoid family and bully
  goblin tribes into service; the goblins scout and screen for them.
- Text :: Goblins know to follow a bugbear's nose for plunder; in return, the
  bugbear gets an obedient bodyguard, and a distraction while it maneuvers for
  the ambush.

*** Goblin Boss's Gang

- Id :: =goblin-bosss-gang=
- Members :: Goblin Boss ×1 (leader); Goblin Warrior ×3–6 (member); Goblin
  Minion ×2–4 (minion)
- Why together :: A goblin boss rules its tribe through fear and cunning, and
  the warrior-versus-minion split is the tribe's own pecking order.
- Text :: Through cunning, bribery, or threats, a goblin boss has climbed the
  ranks to command jealous warriors and fawning minions to do his bidding.

*** Hobgoblin Patrol

- Id :: =hobgoblin-patrol=
- Members :: Hobgoblin Captain ×1 (leader); Hobgoblin Warrior ×3–6 (member);
  Worg ×2–3 (pet)
- Why together :: Hobgoblin legions are drilled and disciplined; captains lead
  patrols, and hobgoblins train worgs as trackers and war-mounts.
- Text :: A hobgoblin captain marches his warriors towards glory, worgs loping
  afar to flush out any ambush.

*** Bugbear Ambush

- Id :: =bugbear-ambush=
- Members :: Bugbear Stalker ×2–3 (leader); Goblin Warrior ×2–4 (member)
- Why together :: Bugbears are ambush predators, and goblins make the noise that

*** Worg Pack

- Id :: =worg-pack=
- Members :: Worg ×2–4 (member); Goblin Warrior ×3–5 (pet)
