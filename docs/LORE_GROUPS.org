#+title:     Bundled lore groups
#+language:  en

The Random Encounter generator's Lore-leaning toggle draws from a bundled set of
creature compositions, and each composition carries a one-line description that
the Compendium's detail pane shows under the members list.  The Elm module that
ships the bundle is the source of truth for the data.  This document is the
writer's copy: it keeps the reasoning behind each composition, which has no home
in the code, beside the text that ships.

* Voice

The descriptions read like the sidebars in a Monster Manual rather than the stat
blocks: one sentence, two at most, written by someone who has run the encounter
and enjoyed it.  A few habits keep new entries in step with the old ones:

- The line explains why these creatures are standing together, and then gives
  the reader something to worry about.
- The joke or the threat lands on the reader.  "You" is fair game.
- Use plain words.  The vocabulary a text generator leans on (delve, realm,
  tapestry, testament, and their kin) is out, and so is the "not X but Y"
  construction.
- Punctuation stays quiet: a semicolon or a colon now and then, an em dash
  rarely, and no lists of three for their own sake.
- Spelling is American, as in the source books.

* Groups

The headings follow the sections in the module.

** Goblinoids

*** Goblinoid Warband

- Id :: =goblinoid-warband=
- Members :: Goblin Warrior ×3–6 (member); Hobgoblin Warrior ×1–2 (leader)
- Why together :: Hobgoblins are the goblinoid soldiery and goblins their
  numerous, expendable kin; Maglubiyet's pecking order puts the one over the
  other.
- Text :: Hobgoblin warriors press goblin levies into a respectable warband —
  the goblins serve as scouts and arrow-fodder for their better-trained elder
  cousins.

*** Goblin Raiding Party

- Id :: =goblin-raid=
- Members :: Goblin Warrior ×3–5 (member); Bugbear Warrior ×1–2 (leader)
- Why together :: Bugbears are the heavies of the goblinoid family and bully
  goblin tribes into service; the goblins scout and screen for them.
- Text :: Goblins know to follow a bugbear's nose for plunder; in return, the
  bugbear gets an obedient bodyguard, and a distraction while it maneuvers for
  the ambush.

*** Goblin Boss's Gang

- Id :: =goblin-bosss-gang=
- Members :: Goblin Boss ×1 (leader); Goblin Warrior ×3–6 (member); Goblin
  Minion ×2–4 (minion)
- Why together :: A goblin boss rules its tribe through fear and cunning, and
  the warrior-versus-minion split is the tribe's own pecking order.
- Text :: Through cunning, bribery, or threats, a goblin boss has climbed the
  ranks to command jealous warriors and fawning minions to do his bidding.

*** Hobgoblin Patrol

- Id :: =hobgoblin-patrol=
- Members :: Hobgoblin Captain ×1 (leader); Hobgoblin Warrior ×3–6 (member);
  Worg ×2–3 (pet)
- Why together :: Hobgoblin legions are drilled and disciplined; captains lead
  patrols, and hobgoblins train worgs as trackers and war-mounts.
- Text :: A hobgoblin captain marches his warriors towards glory, worgs loping
  afar to flush out any ambush.

*** Bugbear Ambush

- Id :: =bugbear-ambush=
- Members :: Bugbear Stalker ×2–3 (leader); Goblin Warrior ×2–4 (member)
- Why together :: Bugbears are ambush predators, and goblins make the noise that
  draws the eye away from them.
- Text :: Goblins distract while the bugbears spring the trap.

*** Worg Pack

- Id :: =worg-pack=
- Members :: Worg ×2–4 (member); Goblin Warrior ×3–5 (pet)
- Why together :: Worgs are wicked, intelligent wolves that tolerate goblin
  riders, and goblins ride them to war.
- Text :: A worg pack lopes through the wild with goblin riders cinched to their
  backs, on the hunt for meat and spoils.

** Dragons and kobolds

*** Kobold Warband

- Id :: =kobold-warband=
- Members :: Kobold Warrior ×6–12 (member)
- Why together :: Kobolds are weak alone and swarm by instinct; pack tactics are
  the whole point of them.
- Text :: Kobolds in packs are dangerous indeed, and a special threat to quarry
  that underestimates them.

*** Young Dragon's Court

- Id :: =wyrmlings-court=
- Members :: Young Red Dragon ×1 (leader); Kobold Warrior ×4–8 (minion)
- Why together :: Kobolds revere dragons as kin and gods and attach themselves
  to any dragon that will have them; a young dragon collects them whether it
  wants to or not.
- Text :: A young red dragon has attracted the attention of fawning kobold
  worshippers; annoying, perhaps, but perhaps useful...

*** Adult Dragon's Tribute

- Id :: =adult-dragon-tribute=
- Members :: Adult Red Dragon ×1 (leader); Kobold Warrior ×8–12 (minion)
- Why together :: The same devotion at scale: an adult dragon's lair is tended
  by kobolds who bring tribute and die for it.
- Text :: An adult red dragon's kobold worshippers will defend their lord to the
  death. Not that their master needs defending, but if their distractions can
  shorten an inconvenient fight, so much the better.

*** Frost Giant Hunt

- Id :: =frost-giant-hunt=

*** Fire Giant's Forge

- Id :: =fire-giant-forge=
- Members :: Fire Giant ×1–2 (leader); Hell Hound ×2–4 (pet)
- Why together :: Fire giants are the master smiths of the volcanic deeps, and

*** Cloud Giant's Eyrie

- Id :: =cloud-giant-eyrie=
- Members :: Cloud Giant ×1 (leader); Griffon ×1–2 (pet)
- Why together :: Cloud giants live for status and keep griffons among their
  flying pets and mounts; a griffon's favorite meal is horse.
- Text :: Griffons prefer horseflesh, but a cloud giant's griffons eat whatever

*** Storm Giant Citadel

- Id :: =storm-giant-citadel=
- Members :: Storm Giant ×1 (leader); Wyvern ×1–2 (pet)
- Why together :: Storm giants withdraw to remote mountain and cloud citadels
  and read the weather for omens; wyverns nest in the same high crags.
- Text :: A storm giant reads omens in the thunderheads; the wyverns on its
  battlements read only the wind, and whatever comes riding it.

*** Ogre Marauders

- Id :: =ogre-marauders=
- Members :: Ogre ×2–3 (member); Bandit ×2–4 (minion)
- Why together :: An ogre follows anyone who keeps it fed, and bandits use ogres

- Text :: Bandits who can keep an ogre fed have the strongest arm on the road,

*** Troll Lair

- Id :: =troll-lair=
- Members :: Troll ×1–2 (member); Ogre Zombie ×0–2 (minion)
- Why together :: Trolls eat anything and leave the rest to rot; in a lair
  steeped in that much death, or under a necromancer's hand, the leftovers rise.
- Text :: A troll seldom finishes a meal, and in the wrong sort of cave the
  leftovers get up again.

** Undead

- Id :: =mummy-tomb=
- Members :: Mummy ×1 (leader); Skeleton ×3–6 (member); Zombie ×2–4 (member)
- Why together :: Mummies are made to guard tombs, and the retainers buried with
  a noble rise as skeletons and zombies to keep the same vigil.
- Text :: The household was buried with its master so it could serve him in the
  next world; as it turned out, the next world was this one.

*** Mummy Lord's Court

- Id :: =mummy-lord-court=
- Members :: Mummy Lord ×1 (leader); Mummy ×1–2 (member); Skeleton ×6–8 (minion)
- Why together :: A mummy lord is a priest-king preserved by ritual, its heart
  hidden away; it keeps a court of mummies and lesser undead and goes on
  reigning.
- Text :: A mummy lord never died so much as adjourned. Break the seal, and
  court is back in session.

*** Lich's Demesne

- Id :: =lich-demesne=
- Members :: Lich ×1 (leader); Ghoul ×2–4 (member); Ghast ×1–2 (member)
- Why together :: A lich wants servants it need not feed, pay, or trust; ghouls
  and the ghasts that lead them dispose of intruders and ask for nothing.
- Text :: A lich has no further use for flesh, so it pays its ghouls in whatever
  walks through the door.

*** Vampire's Brood

- Id :: =vampire-brood=
- Members :: Vampire ×1 (leader); Vampire Spawn ×2–4 (member); Vampire Familiar
  ×1–2 (pet)
- Why together :: A vampire's spawn are bound to its will; its familiars are
  living mortals who serve by day in the hope of being turned, and lose their
  gifts if the master is destroyed.
- Text :: The spawn serve because they have no choice; the familiars serve
  because they've been promised the bite themselves, someday, if they keep
  proving useful.

*** Ghost Haunting

- Id :: =ghost-haunting=
- Members :: Ghost ×1 (leader); Shadow ×1–3 (member); Specter ×1–2 (member)
- Why together :: A ghost is held by unfinished business, and those who die in
  its haunt rise as shadows and specters alongside it.
- Text :: A ghost's unfinished business has a way of becoming other people's.
  The shadows and specters drifting after it are what became of the last few who
  came to settle it.

*** Skeleton Legion

- Id :: =skeleton-legion=
- Members :: Skeleton ×6–10 (member); Warhorse Skeleton ×1–3 (pet); Minotaur
  Skeleton ×0–1 (leader)
- Why together :: Skeletons obey their last command indefinitely; an army raised
  for a war keeps marching long after the war, cavalry and champion included.
- Text :: A skeleton keeps its last order until something breaks it, and this
  legion's last order was to advance.

*** Zombie Horde

- Id :: =zombie-horde=
- Members :: Zombie ×6–12 (member); Ogre Zombie ×1–2 (leader)
- Why together :: Zombies shamble toward the living with no thought at all, and
  an ogre zombie is only a bigger one.
- Text :: A horde has no plan and no general, only a direction; the ogre zombies
  out front are simply the ones with the longest stride.

*** Wraith Host

- Id :: =wraith-host=
- Members :: Wraith ×1 (leader); Specter ×2–4 (member)
- Why together :: A wraith raises the humanoids it kills as specters under its
  control, so its host is made of its own victims.
- Text :: Every specter trailing a wraith was a person once, and every one of
  them met the wraith the same way you're about to.

** Hags

*** Hag Coven

- Id :: =hag-coven=

- Why together :: Hags are solitary and loathe each other, and three form a

- Text :: Hags can't stand one another, which is what makes a coven so worrying:

*** Sea Hag's Tide

- Why together :: Sea hags haunt foul coasts and feed on horror and despair;

*** Green Hag's Grove

- Id :: =green-hags-grove=

*** Night Hag's Bargain

- Why together :: Night hags are fiends that harvest souls into a soul bag for
