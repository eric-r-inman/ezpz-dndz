#+title:     Condition/Effect presets
#+language:  en

The Condition/Effect editor ships presets for effects from the 2024 rules.  This
document records where a preset falls short of its source — what the rule asks
that the editor cannot carry — and what supporting it would take.  The presets
themselves are the source of truth for their settings.

The sources are SRD 5.2.1 wherever an effect appears there.  Several do not: the
Battle Master maneuvers, Wrathful and Staggering Smite, and Cause Fear come from
the 2024 Player's Handbook; the mind flayer, beholder, and carrion crawler from
the 2024 Monster Manual; quicksand from the 2024 Dungeon Master's Guide; and
Hexblade's Curse from Xanathar's Guide to Everything.  Those presets follow the
books as recalled rather than as checked, and are marked /unverified/ below.

* Where the editor falls short

A preset holds what the editor's form holds.  A custom-named preset's note
carries a reminder of whatever else the rule asks, where one fits; a preset that
applies a standard condition carries no note.  Each gap names presets that meet
it — examples rather than a census — and how much supporting it would take.
/Simple/ is a setting or two of a shape the editor already has, /moderate/ is
new state the editor and the encounter must both carry, and /complex/ is a
mechanic the tracker does not model at all.

** Timing an effect off its source's turn — simple

Many effects last until the start or end of the caster's or attacker's next
turn.  A preset cannot name that creature, so these presets run to the end of
the target's own next turn, which covers the turn the effect is meant to cost
the target but can keep it a little past the source's.  The GM can aim the
duration at the source creature before applying.  Supporting it takes a preset
setting that aims the duration at the creature whose turn it is.

- Stunning Strike (Monk), Menacing Attack (unverified), Goading Attack
  (unverified), Staggering Smite (unverified)
- Mind Blast (Mind Flayer) (unverified), Horrific Visage (Ghost)

** Ending on damage, without a save — simple

Some effects end the moment the bearer takes damage, or damage from the caster's
side.  The editor's damage settings belong to a save, which they roll or flag,
and these rules want neither, so the GM removes the chip when the trigger lands.
Supporting it takes an "ends when damaged" choice that needs no save, with the
source of the damage left to the GM.

- Turn Undead, Sleep, Hypnotic Pattern, Sleep Ray (Beholder) (unverified)
- Charm Person, Suggestion, Vampire Charm, and Staff of Charming, which end on
  damage from the caster or the caster's allies

** Damage every turn, whatever the save — simple

Searing Smite burns its target at the start of each of the target's turns before
the save, Ensnaring Strike deals piercing damage each turn the target stays
restrained, and a burning creature takes fire damage at the start of each of its
turns.  The failed-save damage setting fires only on a failure, so the GM deals
that damage by hand; Searing Smite and On Fire name it in their note.
Supporting it takes a damage setting that fires on the bearer's turn rather than
on the save.

- Searing Smite, Ensnaring Strike, On Fire

** Escape checks rather than saves — simple

Web, Entangle, Black Tentacles, Ensnaring Strike, a net, and quicksand end when
a creature spends an action on a Strength (Athletics) check, and the roper's
tentacle when its grapple is escaped.  The presets stand a manual save in for
the check: the GM rolls it from the chip when the attempt is made, adding the
creature's Athletics bonus, and a success removes the condition.  The ability
picker offers only the six abilities, so the check reads as a Strength save.
Supporting it takes a check beside the save, naming the skill it uses.

- Web, Entangle, Black Tentacles, Ensnaring Strike
- Tendril (Roper), Net, Quicksand (unverified)

Black Tentacles also calls for a Strength save from a restrained creature that
ends its turn in the area, which decides only whether the tentacles deal their
damage again.  A save that cannot end the condition needs a setting of its own,
a moderate change.

** Durations beyond the countdown — simple

The countdown runs to 99 of the bearer's turns, just short of ten minutes, and
no setting rolls a duration.  Presets for effects that last longer, or for a
rolled number of minutes, last until removed, and the GM keeps the clock.
Supporting it takes a longer countdown, and one that can roll its length.

- Shield of Faith, Spike Growth, Hex, Hunter's Mark, Pass Without Trace, Wild
  Shape
- Web, Charm Person, Suggestion, Vampire Charm
- Staff of Charming, Potion of Invisibility, Potion of Heroism, Potion of Giant
  Strength, Potion of Climbing, Dust of Disappearance

** Concentration — moderate

Most spell effects end when their caster's concentration does.  A condition
keeps no link to its caster, so the GM removes the chip when concentration ends;
some custom-named presets' notes say "conc" as the reminder.  The companion
link, which already ends one condition with another on the same creature, is the
natural start; this one would have to reach across to the caster's Concentrating
status.

- Bless, Hex, Hunter's Mark, Guidance, Shield of Faith, Spike Growth, Shining
  Smite, Pass Without Trace, Ensnaring Strike, Haste, Heroism
- Bane, Hold Person, Hold Monster, Sleep, Cause Fear (unverified), Fear,
  Hypnotic Pattern, Hideous Laughter, Suggestion, Slow, Web, Entangle, Black
  Tentacles, Faerie Fire, Banishment, Greater Invisibility
- Frightful Presence (Dragon), Luring Song (Harpy), Wand of Fear

** Roll modifiers — complex

The tracker does not adjust rolls, so dice added to or taken from attacks,
saves, and checks, changes to AC, and advantage or disadvantage are the GM's to
apply, with a chip's label or note as the reminder where one fits.  Supporting
them means modelling the rolls themselves.

- Bardic Inspiration (+d6, +d8, +d10), Bless, Bane, Guidance, Hex, Hunter's
  Mark, Hexblade's Curse (unverified), Rage, Reckless Attack, Patient Defense
- Shining Smite, Faerie Fire, Pass Without Trace, Vicious Mockery, Goading
  Attack (unverified), Slow, Haste, Shield, Shield of Faith

** Temporary hit points — moderate

Heroism grants temporary hit points equal to its caster's spellcasting modifier
at the start of each of the target's turns, a Potion of Heroism grants 10, and
Wild Shape grants the druid's level in them on taking the form.  The HP editor
sets temporary hit points by hand, and a preset cannot grant them.  Supporting
it takes a grant a condition makes when applied, or on each of the bearer's
turns.

- Heroism, Potion of Heroism, Wild Shape

** Speed, resistances, actions, and ability scores — complex

- Rage's resistances and Strength advantage
- Haste's and Slow's changes to Speed and actions
- Hypnotic Pattern's Speed of 0, which its companion only names
- Stinking Cloud's lost action and Bonus Action
- Potion of Giant Strength's Strength score and Potion of Climbing's climb Speed

** Effects that end on the bearer's own actions — complex

- Potion of Invisibility and Dust of Disappearance end when the bearer makes an
  attack roll, deals damage, or casts a spell.
- Sanctuary ends when the warded creature makes an attack roll, casts a spell,
  or deals damage.
- Vicious Mockery and Bardic Inspiration end with the roll they change.
- Rage lasts only while the barbarian keeps extending it, turn by turn.

** Growing effects — moderate

Suffocating adds a level of Exhaustion at the end of each of the bearer's turns,
and extreme cold or heat adds one on each hour's failed save, without the effect
ending.  A failed save can deal damage or change the condition but cannot add
Exhaustion, so the GM adds the levels, and the custom-named presets' notes carry
the schedule.  Extreme heat's DC also rises each hour, and quicksand's escape DC
rises as the creature sinks.  Supporting it takes an Exhaustion step on a
schedule, beside the failed-save outcomes.

- Drowning, Dust of Sneezing and Choking, Extreme Cold, Extreme Heat, Quicksand
  (unverified)

** Immunity after a successful save — moderate

A creature that succeeds on its save against the ghost's Horrific Visage or the
harpy's Luring Song is immune to it for a day.  The Save Chain editor grants
timed immunity; the Condition/Effect editor does not, so the GM keeps track of
who saved.

- Horrific Visage (Ghost), Luring Song (Harpy)

** Areas and zones — complex

The Save Chain editor tracks the creatures standing in an area.  A condition
preset covers one creature's condition, so a zone's own damage, terrain, and
entry saves stay with the GM, or with a Save Chain preset.

- Spike Growth, Web, Entangle, Black Tentacles, Stinking Cloud, Pass Without
  Trace, Slippery Surface, Heavy Obscurement

** Outcomes the editor has no setting for — complex

- Web (Giant Spider), Tendril (Roper), and Net end when the web, tentacle, or
  net, an object with its own AC and hit points, is destroyed.
- Banishment keeps some extraplanar targets away for good when the spell lasts
  its full minute.
- Haste's ending costs its target the next turn.
- Command's Grovel knocks the target Prone and ends its turn.

** Instant effects

Pushing Attack (unverified) and Sneak Attack leave nothing lasting.  Their
presets clear themselves when the bearer's turn next ends: a Pushed chip on the
creature pushed, and a reminder on the rogue that Sneak Attack is spent for the
turn.

* Readings to confirm

- Hideous Laughter :: The 2024 spell gives its target the Prone condition for
  the spell's duration and keeps the target from ending it, so this preset ends
  Prone with the spell.  The Save Chain preset for the same spell leaves the
  target Prone until it stands, which reads like the 2014 wording.  The two
  should agree, and the 2024 wording favours this one.
- Heroism :: The Heroism preset follows the spell, as the request that added it
  described: a minute of immunity to Frightened.  Potion of Heroism shares the
  name but not the effect — 10 temporary hit points and the Bless spell for an
  hour, with no immunity — so its preset keeps to the potion, and its chip reads
  Potion of Heroism.
- Pit Trap :: The SRD's hidden pit deals falling damage, and a creature that
  lands from a fall is Prone, so the preset marks the creature In Pit with Prone
  beside it rather than Restrained.

* Updating stored presets

A stored copy of a bundled preset outranks the shipped one, so the presets the
app seeded before these corrections keep their old settings and names.  Restore
bundled presets, at the bottom of the Load menu, lays the shipped presets over
the stored ones, drops the names the bundle has retired, and leaves the GM's own
presets alone.
