#+title:     Save Chain presets
#+language:  en

The Save Chain editor's bundled presets each transcribe one saving-throw effect
from the 2024 rules.  This document is the review behind them: what the source
text says, what the editor can express today, and the settings it still needs.
The Elm module that ships the presets is the source of truth for the data; the
backlog entry "Teach the Save Chain editor the rules its presets need" in
tasks.org tracks the settings.

Sources are SRD 5.2.1 (the 2024 rules) unless an entry says otherwise.  Acid
Splash and Toll the Dead are not in the SRD and follow the 2024 Player's
Handbook.

* What a preset can express

A chain names the save ability, optionally a fixed DC, and one outcome for each
side of the save.  An outcome may deal damage (a dice formula or an integer),
drain (damage that also lowers the hit point maximum by the amount dealt), heal,
or — on the success side — deal half of the fail damage, and it may attach any
number of named effects.  Each effect carries a duration in the Condition
editor's terms — until removed, until the start or end of a creature's next turn
(the target's, the active creature's, or a named one's), until the end of the
target's current turn, a countdown of turns, or one minute — and may name a
companion condition applied beside it that ends when it ends.  An effect may opt
into Save-to-end, which gives the applied condition the chain's ability and DC
and one of four timings: manual (a 🎲 on the chip), at the start of the bearer's
turn, at the end of it, or "ask at end of turn", where the chip flashes as the
turn ends and the GM rolls if the rule's trigger applied; a success ends the
condition, and a failure may deal damage again or turn the condition into
another, which ends the saving.  Taking damage may also flash the chip for the
GM to judge, or roll the save at once, plain or with advantage.  A successful
save may grant the target immunity to the chain for a chosen duration, which the
chain honours by passing over that creature.  An area effect — Cloudkill, Spirit
Guardians — marks each target with an "In:" chip that rolls the chain's save
again at the start or end of each of that creature's turns and applies the side
the roll earns, with a 🎲 for a creature that enters mid-turn and a × for one
that leaves.  Anything else an applied effect needs lives in its note for the GM
to act on.

Verdicts below: /exact/ when the editor models the rule fully, /approximate/
when the note carries what it can't, /retired/ when the effect is no longer a
save.

* Cantrips

** Acid Splash

- Rule :: Dexterity save; 1d6 acid damage on a failure, nothing on a success.
- Preset :: DEX; fail 1d6.
- Verdict :: exact.

** Sacred Flame

- Rule :: Dexterity save; 1d8 radiant damage on a failure, nothing on a success;
  no benefit from half or three-quarters cover.
- Preset :: DEX; fail 1d8.
- Verdict :: exact.

** Toll the Dead

- Rule :: Wisdom save; 1d8 necrotic damage, or 1d12 if the target is missing any
  hit points.
- Preset :: WIS; fail 1d8.
- Verdict :: approximate — the GM overwrites the amount for a wounded target.

** Poison Spray

- Rule :: A ranged spell attack for 1d12 poison damage.
- Verdict :: retired — the 2024 cantrip has no save.

* Level 1

** Bane

- Rule :: Charisma save; a failing target subtracts 1d4 from attack rolls and
  saves until concentration ends, with no repeat save.
- Preset :: CHA; fail applies "Baned" with no Save-to-end.
- Verdict :: exact — the −1d4 is the GM's to apply at each roll.

** Burning Hands

- Rule :: Dexterity save; 3d6 fire, half on a success.
- Preset :: DEX; fail 3d6, success half.
- Verdict :: exact.

** Command

- Rule :: Wisdom save; the target follows the one-word command on its next turn.
- Preset :: WIS; fail applies "Commanded" until the end of the target's next
  turn.
- Verdict :: exact.

** Hideous Laughter

- Rule :: Wisdom save; Prone and Incapacitated for the duration, repeating the
  save at the end of each of its turns and whenever it takes damage (with
  advantage on the damage-triggered save).
- Preset :: WIS; fail applies Incapacitated with an end-of-turn Save-to-end that
  also rolls with advantage whenever the target takes damage, and Prone without
  one.
- Verdict :: exact — Prone outliving the laughter until the target stands is the
  rule.

** Sleep

- Rule :: Wisdom save; Incapacitated until the end of its next turn, then a
  repeat save; a second failure makes it Unconscious for the duration.  Damage
  or a shake ends the spell.
- Preset :: WIS; fail applies Incapacitated for a minute with an end-of-turn
  Save-to-end whose failure makes it Unconscious.
- Verdict :: exact — damage or a shake ending it early is the GM's to apply.

** Thunderwave

- Rule :: Constitution save; 2d8 thunder and a 10-foot push, half damage and no
  push on a success.
- Preset :: CON; fail 2d8, success half.
- Verdict :: approximate — the push is repositioning the GM handles.

* Level 2

** Blindness / Deafness

- Rule :: Constitution save; Blinded or Deafened for 1 minute, repeating the
  save at the end of each of its turns.
- Preset :: CON; fail applies Blinded with an end-of-turn Save-to-end.
- Verdict :: exact — the GM renames the effect when the caster chose Deafened.

** Hold Person

- Rule :: Wisdom save; Paralyzed for the duration, repeating at the end of each
  of its turns.
- Preset :: WIS; fail applies Paralyzed with an end-of-turn Save-to-end.
- Verdict :: exact.

** Ray of Enfeeblement

- Rule :: Constitution save.  Failure: disadvantage on Strength-based D20 tests
  and −1d8 from damage rolls, repeating at the end of each of its turns.
  Success: disadvantage on its next attack roll until the start of the caster's
  next turn.
- Preset :: CON; fail applies "Enfeebled" with an end-of-turn Save-to-end;
  success applies "Disadv next attack" until the start of the active creature's
  next turn.
- Verdict :: exact.

** Shatter

- Rule :: Constitution save; 3d8 thunder, half on a success; a construct has
  disadvantage.
- Preset :: CON; fail 3d8, success half.
- Verdict :: exact — Roll Disadv. covers constructs.

** Suggestion

- Rule :: Wisdom save; Charmed for up to 8 hours of concentration, ending if the
  caster or an ally damages the target.
- Preset :: WIS; fail applies Charmed with the spell in its note.
- Verdict :: approximate — the duration lives in the note.

** Web

- Rule :: Dexterity save; Restrained while in the webs; an action to make a
  Strength (Athletics) check against the spell DC frees the target.
- Preset :: DEX; fail applies Restrained with the escape check in its note.
- Verdict :: exact — the escape is a check, not a save.

** Spirit Guardians

- Rule :: Wisdom save when the emanation enters a creature's space or the
  creature enters it or ends its turn there, once per turn; 3d8 radiant or
  necrotic, half on a success.
- Preset :: WIS; fail 3d8, success half; an area effect rolled at the end of

  mid-turn.
- Verdict :: exact.

** Stinking Cloud

- Rule :: Constitution save at the start of each turn in the cloud; on a
  failure, Poisoned until the end of that turn and unable to take an action or
  bonus action.
- Preset :: CON; an area effect rolled at the start of each marked creature's
  turn; fail applies Poisoned until the end of that turn.
- Verdict :: exact — the GM removes the "In:" chip when the creature leaves the
  cloud.

* Level 4

** Banishment

- Rule :: Charisma save; banished and Incapacitated until concentration ends,
  with no repeat save.
- Preset :: CHA; fail applies "Banished" with no Save-to-end.
- Verdict :: exact — the 2024 text grants no repeat save.

** Blight

- Rule :: Constitution save; 8d8 necrotic, half on a success; a Plant creature

- Verdict :: exact — the Fail button covers plants.

** Confusion

- Rule :: Wisdom save; acts by the d10 table each turn, repeating the save at

** Ice Storm

- Rule :: Dexterity save; 2d10 bludgeoning and 4d6 cold, half on a success.

** Phantasmal Killer

- Rule :: Wisdom save.  Failure: 4d10 psychic and disadvantage on ability checks
  and attack rolls for the duration.  Success: half damage and the spell ends.
  At the end of each of its turns the target repeats the save, taking the damage
  again on a failure and ending the spell on a success.
- Preset :: WIS; fail 4d10 and "Nightmare" with an end-of-turn Save-to-end that
  deals 4d10 again on a failure; success half.
- Verdict :: exact — the 2024 spell no longer frightens, where the 2014 one did.

* Monster abilities

** Ghoul Claw

- Rule :: Constitution save, DC 10; Paralyzed until the end of its next turn.
  Undead and elves are unaffected.
- Preset :: CON 10; fail applies Paralyzed until the end of the target's next
  turn.
- Verdict :: exact.

** Ghast Stench

- Rule :: Constitution save, DC 10, for any creature that starts its turn within
  5 feet of the ghast.  Failure: Poisoned until the start of its next turn.
  Success: immune to this ghast's stench for 24 hours.
- Preset :: CON 10; an area effect rolled at the start of each marked creature's
  turn, which passes over an immune creature; fail applies Poisoned until the
  start of the target's next turn; success grants immunity to the chain.
- Verdict :: approximate — the immunity lasts until removed rather than 24
  hours.

** Harpy Luring Song

- Rule :: Wisdom save, DC 11, when the song starts.  Failure: Charmed until the
  song ends, repeating the save at the end of each of its turns and whenever
  another source damages it; while Charmed it is Incapacitated and moves toward
  the harpy.  Success: immune to this harpy's song for 24 hours.
- Preset :: WIS 11; fail applies Charmed, with Incapacitated as its companion,
  with an end-of-turn Save-to-end whose chip flashes whenever the target takes
  damage for the GM to judge the source; success grants immunity to the chain.
- Verdict :: approximate — the immunity lasts until removed rather than 24
  hours.

** Mummy Dreadful Glare

- Rule :: Wisdom save, DC 11.  Failure: Frightened until the end of the mummy's
  next turn.  Success: immune to this mummy's glare for 24 hours.
- Preset :: WIS 11; fail applies Frightened until the end of the active
  creature's next turn; success grants immunity to the chain.
- Verdict :: approximate — the immunity lasts until removed rather than 24
  hours.

** Medusa Petrifying Gaze

- Rule :: Constitution save, DC 13.  First failure: Restrained, repeating the
  save at the end of its next turn.  Second failure: Petrified.
- Preset :: CON 13; fail applies Restrained with an end-of-turn Save-to-end

- Verdict :: exact.

** Wight Life Drain

- Rule :: Constitution save, DC 13.  Failure: 1d8 + 2 necrotic, and the hit
  point maximum drops by the damage taken.  Success: half damage only.
- Preset :: CON 13; fail drains 1d8+2, success half.
- Verdict :: exact.

* What stays approximate

The settings the review asked for have landed, and the verdicts above are the
state of the editor today.  The 24-hour immunities a ghast, a harpy, and a mummy
grant last until removed, since a fight never reaches the next day; the rest of
the /approximate/ verdicts — a wounded target's larger cantrip damage, a push, a
duration the note carries — are single-effect quirks the GM handles at the table
rather than settings worth a control.  The backlog entry "Teach the Save Chain
editor the rules its presets need" in tasks.org records why threshold outcomes
stay a note as well.
